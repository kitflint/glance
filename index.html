Made for human eyes.
Built for clear decisions.

Glance is a free, local-first project board that shows where a project stands at a glance: its stage, focus, next actions, evidence and what a human has actually reviewed. No account needed to start. No ads. No trackers.

Download v0.1.0 pre-release Read the Quickstart

Your board, on your machine

Edit tasks, criteria and evidence locally. Save, reopen and back up at the same revision.

Bring your own AI

Local MCP tools let your assistant read the board and, if you opt in, make guarded updates. It can never record human acceptance.

GitHub, both ways, if you want

Import issues, pull requests and checks with their freshness, or publish reviewed issue changes after an exact preview.

Honest about what it is

  • Version 0.1.0 is tested on Windows 11 x64 with Node.js 24. Other platforms are untested.
  • Pre-release usability walkthroughs were simulated, not run with real users. Your feedback is the first independent test.
